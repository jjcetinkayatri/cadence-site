Common questions

  • Sign-in with Google or Apple isn't working

    Make sure you're connected to the internet and using the latest version of the app from the App Store. If a sign-in sheet doesn't open or closes immediately, force-quit Cadence and try again — if that doesn't help, email us with your device model and iOS version.

  • My Strava data isn't syncing

    Open Settings inside Cadence and check that Strava is still connected — Strava access can expire and need reconnecting. New activities can take a few minutes to appear after you finish a Strava sync.

  • A race prediction looks wrong

    Predictions improve as more of your training history syncs in, and factor in course elevation and surface — a result can look off early on, or for a race type you haven't run before. Tell us the race and what looked wrong and we'll take a look.

  • I want my data exported or deleted

    Email us from the address associated with your account and we'll export or permanently delete your data, usually within a few business days.
