First release

In App Review

Submitted October 2026

  • Physiology-based race predictions. A real baseline model, not a flat distance multiplier, corrected against your own training history and course elevation.
  • Strava auto-sync. Connect once and your power curve, long runs, and rides stay current automatically, with no manual re-entry.
  • Season planning. See your whole season at once, with deterministic taper guidance heading into your peak race.
  • Voice and text commands. Add a race, drop one, or pull up a fit report by asking directly, no menu-hunting.
  • Free and Season plans. Prediction accuracy — confidence tier, limiter, and predicted range — is identical on both. Season adds unlimited race tracking, Strava auto-sync, and season planning.
  • Race catalog. Running and cycling (road, trail, and gravel included), covering the US and Canada.
What's next: we're already working on what comes after launch, including broader race coverage and sharper predictions. Check back here as new updates ship.
