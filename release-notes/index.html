Release Notes

What's shipped in Cadence, and what we're working on next. We'll keep this updated as new versions go out.

Second release

  • Strava is gone. Cadence now works from the numbers you type and the race results you confirm. Anything that earlier synced from Strava stays in your profile, labelled “Synced earlier”, and your stored Strava data has been deleted.
  • A dated Season plan. The four taper steps for your focus race now carry dates, and every tracked race shows its recovery window, with a warning when a tune-up’s recovery runs into your taper.
  • Race-day targets are part of Season. Pace and per-km splits for a run, or steady power and speed for a ride, and the Goal screen’s “what your goal needs”. Your predicted range stays free.
  • See how often you finish inside the range. A “7 of 9 finished inside the range” card on Insights for every plan, and one optional question after a finish about how hard you went, so an easy run counts for less.

Released October 2026

  • Physiology-based race predictions. A real baseline model, not a flat distance multiplier, corrected against your own training history and course elevation.
  • Strava auto-sync. Connect once and your power curve, long runs, and rides refresh whenever you open the app, with no manual re-entry.
  • Season planning. See your whole season at once, with deterministic taper guidance heading into your peak race.
  • Voice and text commands. Add a race, drop one, or pull up a fit report by asking directly, no menu-hunting.
  • Free and Season plans. The prediction model is the same on both: the same inputs give the same confidence tier, limiter, and predicted range. Season adds unlimited race tracking, Strava auto-sync, season planning, and your track record.
  • Race catalog. Running and cycling (road, trail, and gravel included), covering the US and Canada, plus a hand-picked set across Europe, the UK, Australia and New Zealand.
