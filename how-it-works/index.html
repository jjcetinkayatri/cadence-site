How it works

From "will this race fit me" to a plan you trust.

Real moments in the app, not a feature list: getting your data in, a straight verdict on a race, a taper that adjusts itself, asking out loud instead of hunting through menus, and alerts when a new race fits you.

Step 3

Taper without guessing

Set a peak race and Cadence lays out a deterministic taper — four phases built from how many weeks out you are. Never an AI-generated plan, and it tells you plainly which phase you're in right now.

Race week

Volume bottoms out, 60–80% below peak. Rested, not undertrained.

Step 5

It tells you when a new race fits

When Cadence refreshes the race catalog, it checks every new race against your fitness. A race that scores a real fit becomes a notification, so you don't have to go looking for it. Open the app now and then to keep the check running.
